# competitive-brief

## Purpose

Turn public evidence into a decision-oriented competitive intelligence brief.

## Framework

1. define the decision question
2. identify comparison axes
3. gather target and competitor evidence
4. evaluate source quality
5. distinguish evidence from inference
6. produce a brief with implications and risks

## Required output

- executive summary
- key findings
- target or competitor breakdown
- implications
- risks and unknowns
- evidence notes

## Guardrails

- do not confuse positioning copy with verified market reality
- do not suppress uncertainty when strategic interpretation is thin
- keep the brief decision-useful, not encyclopedic
