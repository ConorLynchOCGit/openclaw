# Research Handoff Contract

## Purpose

Define the handoff between `web-researcher` and `writer` so evidence gathering and evidence packaging stay cleanly separated.

## Use When

- the writing task depends on fresh or external evidence
- the writer is working from a delegated research result

## Required Inputs From Web Researcher

- objective
- source basis
- key findings
- important qualifiers
- unresolved points
- URLs or citation notes

## Writer Rules

- do not strengthen claims beyond the handoff evidence
- preserve qualifiers that matter to the decision
- surface gaps instead of filling them with style
- optimize the final artifact for the target audience

## Escalate Back To Research When

- a key claim lacks support
- freshness matters and the evidence may be stale
- contradictions remain unresolved
