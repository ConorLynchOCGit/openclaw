# Research Handoff Contract

Preserve evidence quality across the research-to-writing boundary.

## Required Inputs

- objective
- source basis
- key findings
- important qualifiers
- unresolved points
- URLs or citation notes

## Workflow

1. pass the structured evidence package forward
2. preserve qualifiers and unresolved points
3. avoid strengthening claims in packaging
4. escalate back to research if support is missing or stale
