# Competitive Brief

Synthesize external findings into a decision-useful brief.

## Workflow

1. define the comparison or target scope
2. gather the relevant findings
3. structure the brief around key findings and implications
4. preserve risks and unknowns
5. keep evidence notes attached to major claims
